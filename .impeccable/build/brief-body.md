# Home — portfolio de Valentino Araya

Scope: the single-page home (`src/App.tsx`): hero, about, projects, closing contact/footer. Visitor mode: Persuade (the visitor decides whether to interview or hire).

Audience and job: recruiters and tech leads first, potential clients second. Action: download the CV (primary), then reach out. Proof: five real projects described from `content/projects/`, no screenshots. Constraints: bilingual ES (default) / EN, same facts in both.

Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/comp-a.png` (centered statement hero with project strip).

Visual reference pinned by the user: Extrafazant (light grey ground, ultra-heavy condensed grotesk caps paired with light condensed serif caps, cobalt blue accent, pink on dark sections, white pill nav, tilted polaroid photos with a die-cut sticker).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A developer portfolio that talks like a studio poster, not a résumé template: one loud claim ("Construyo software que está en producción") backed by the names of shipped products. Refuses the dark-mode terminal hero and the grid of equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Pale grey paper ground (#f8f8f7), near-black ink, cobalt blue (#0836f3) as the only action colour on light; a near-black section where hot pink (#ff8fd0) takes over the action role. Type: Anton caps against Instrument Serif caps on the same headline, Archivo for reading text, a blue brush-script wordmark. Controls are square arrow tiles joined to label tiles; nav is a white pill.

STORY: In one viewport the visitor learns who he is, that his software is live, and where the CV is. The about section puts a face and a desk (mate included) behind the claim. The projects index proves it product by product, then the dark close asks for the conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Pill nav centered at top, wordmark top-left, language and contact chips top-right. Centered: name line in serif, two-line headline spanning ~70% width (grotesk over serif), one sans line of facts, the blue "DESCARGAR CV" button at the optical center below. Bottom edge: a static five-column strip of the project names, each in its own lettering and each a link to its entry.

FORM: Pinned by the user's reference; the roll was skipped because the user pinned the direction. Comp A of three, approved. seed: none (roll skipped: direction pinned by user reference)
